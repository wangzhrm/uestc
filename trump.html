Excerpt from a Speech

Friends, citizens, patriots. We stand here today at a critical moment for our nation's prosperity. The US economy has grown at an incredible rate since our policies were enacted. This is a testament to our strength and our resilience. But we cannot rest on our laurels. We must continue to put American workers first when negotiating trade deals. Our commitment to them is unwavering. I'm also proud to announce that the statistics don't lie. Trade deficits have decreased by 20% over the last year. These are the kinds of results we promised. Of course, our work is not just about economics. It is essential that we build a strong border wall to ensure our national security. It is a matter of safety for all of our families. We've also seen incredible progress on the jobs front. The unemployment rate is at a historic low of 3.5% nationwide. We are making America strong again!
